      Listen port
      —
      The port your VPN provider forwards to you. Peers can only start a connection to you on this port — without it you can reach only those peers who happen to have an open port themselves, which on a small swarm is often none of them. Takes effect after a restart.
      —
      Upload limit
      Capping upload usually makes downloads faster: a saturated uplink delays the acknowledgements your downloads depend on. Don't set this too low — peers share back with peers who share with them.
      Piece strategy
      Auto downloads in order while your buffer is thin, then switches to rarest-first, which earns you more peers. Only change this to compare the two.
      Encrypt peer connections
      —
      Obfuscates the BitTorrent protocol so an ISP has a harder time recognising and throttling it. This is not privacy — every peer still sees your address. Only TCP peers can use it; uTP peers stay in the clear. Takes effect after a restart, in both directions.
      Playback
      Read track durations
      Reads length from files that have finished downloading. Uses bandwidth and can slow the track you're listening to, so it's off by default. Durations are always learned for free from tracks you play.
